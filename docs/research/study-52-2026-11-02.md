# Study of the legislative chain over the 52 acts of #617

**Status:** research result, 2026-11-02. The release state stays `research_only`.
Nothing is published, and nothing here is a coverage claim beyond this declared set.

**Authority:** ADR-0017, [#610](https://github.com/bartoszkobylinski/lovspor/issues/610)
workstream B, building on the [E4 pilot](pilot-e4-2026-11-01.md) and the
[#617 source-first audit](lovverk-first-100-audit-2026-10-14.md).
**Derived results:** [`data/study-52-2026-11-02.json`](data/study-52-2026-11-02.json)
(every record, with hashes, URLs and statuses) and
[`data/study-52-2026-11-02.csv`](data/study-52-2026-11-02.csv) (one row per act).
**Code:** `scripts/study_52.py`, `src/lovspor/stortinget_discovery.py`,
`read_lovtidend_members` and `cited_odelsting_decisions` in `src/lovspor/legislative_chain.py`.

## What was measured

For each act, the chain is LOV act → decision → Stortinget sak, built from two
independent official sources:

1. **Law edge (Lovdata publicData).** The act's document is read from the Lovtidend
   avd. I archive. Its three header identities must match the act. The decision
   comes **only** from the header field `Annet om dokumentet`.
2. **Case discovery (data.stortinget.no), label-free.** For a header decision
   *Lovvedtak N (S)*, the session's Lovvedtak publication list confirms that id
   `vedtak-SSSSss-NNN` exists. The sak listings for S and the session before S
   propose candidates whose free-text `henvisning` names N, by an exact parse of
   singles, ranges and lists. Each candidate is fetched, and a sak is accepted
   only if its own typed `publikasjon_referanse` (`type` lovvedtak) names the
   decision by both link text and `eksport_id`. One confirmation means
   `discovered`, two or more mean `ambiguous`, and none means `not_found`. A
   candidate or listing that was not read gives `incomplete`. No title, date or
   fuzzy join is used, and no LLM.

   **Evidence policy for the publication list: mandatory.** The session's
   Lovvedtak publication list is a required, independent confirmation that the
   decision exists. A typed case reference alone never discovers. A case that
   would otherwise be `discovered` becomes:
   - `incomplete` (chain `unresolved`) when the list was not read or did not
     parse (`publication_listed: null`);
   - `unlisted` (chain `contradicted`) when the list was read and does not hold
     the decision's id (`publication_listed: false`).

   Neither can become `attested`. Other outcomes pass unchanged. A listing
   answered with HTTP 200 whose body is not the expected XML counts as not read,
   never as an empty listing. Its parse error is kept beside its URL, digest and
   status under `decisions[].listings[].parse_error`, and the decisions that need
   it come out `incomplete`.
3. **Known-label verification, a separate pass.** Each manually located #617 sak id
   is fetched again on its own, and `resolve_chain` runs with the header decision
   as the expected value.
4. **Evaluation**, after the fact: discovery is compared with the labels.

The discovery path never reads the label file
`lovverk-first-52-proceedings-2026-10-14.csv`. Its inputs are the
source-extraction CSV and the Lovtidend headers. The verification pass and the
evaluation read the labels only after discovery has finished, in both the
capture and the build. `test_discovery_runs_with_the_labels_unreadable` runs
discovery with the label path pointing at a missing file.

Out of scope, never inferred: which § a vote decided, authorship, `valid_at`,
and any `corrects` relation between sanctions.

## Denominators

| Quantity | Count |
|---|---|
| Base laws sampled (Lovverk-first, FNV-1a ordered, strata 35/30/35) | **100** |
| Base laws with an eligible amendment reference | **83** (old 31, mid 27, new 25) |
| Base laws with none | **17** (old 4, mid 3, new 10) |
| Distinct amending acts cited (exact dedup of the 83 references) | **52** |
| Acts found in the Lovtidend archives | 52 / 52 (37 in `2001-2025`, 15 in `2026`) |
| Headers citing exactly one Lovvedtak | **51 / 52** |
| Headers citing Besl. O. | **1 / 52** (LOV-2009-06-19-107, Besl. O. nr. 87 (2008–2009)) |
| Headers citing nothing, or several decisions | 0 / 52 |

The 52 are **not a random sample of amending acts.** They are the distinct acts
that a stratified, FNV-ordered selection of current base laws cites as its most
recent 2007–2026 amendment. The frame favours recent acts: 34 of the 52 are
dated 2025 or 2026, and only 3 predate 2013. The 83 references and the 52 acts
are separate denominators. One act (LOV-2015-06-19-65) is cited by 10 base laws.

## Results

### All outcomes, per act (label-free chain)

| Status | Acts |
|---|---|
| `attested` (header decision, typed and unique case) | **51** |
| `unsupported` (Besl. O., the reader types Lovvedtak only) | **1** |
| `unresolved` / `ambiguous` / `contradicted` | 0 / 0 / 0 |

Edges: law identity 52 `attested`. Law → decision: 51 `attested`, 1 `unsupported`.
Discovery: 51 `discovered`, 1 `not_attempted` (the Besl. O. act). No `not_found`,
`ambiguous` or `incomplete` outcome occurred. Each of the 52 rows is in the
committed files, the unsupported one included.

### Discovery recall against the #617 labels

- **51 / 51 (100 %) of acts whose Lovtidend header cites exactly one Lovvedtak.**
  The discovered sak equals the label sak for all 51, with 0 mismatches and 0
  ambiguous results. The two-sided 95 % Clopper–Pearson lower bound for 51/51 is
  0.930.
- The recall is **conditional** on (a) a header citing exactly one Lovvedtak, and
  (b) the labels being correct.
- **Besl. O. act (1 of 52): recall NOT MEASURED.** Discovery was not attempted.
- **Acts with no header citation: recall NOT MEASURED** (none in this set).
- The **header decision equals the manually read decision for 52 / 52** acts,
  including the Besl. O. act compared with its Besl. O. label.

### Known-label verification (separate pass, separately reported)

- **51 / 51 `attested`** with `resolve_chain`, both edges `attested`, conditional on
  a single header Lovvedtak. This number is not combined with the recall.
- LOV-2009-06-19-107 → sak 43206: `unsupported`. The case was fetched and recorded
  (`sak_sesjon` 2008-2009, no typed `Lovvedtak` reference read by `sak-xml-2`).

### What discovery needed

- **51 / 51** decision ids were present in the session's Lovvedtak publication list.
- **10 of the 51 cases were opened in the session before the decision.** They appear
  only in the previous session's sak listing. Searching only the decision's own
  session would have missed them: 41/51 instead of 51/51. Sak 103533 is one of these.
- 50 decisions had exactly one `henvisning` candidate. One, Lovvedtak 104
  (2024–2025), had three: saker 102086, 102830 and 103123. Only 103123 carries the
  typed reference.
- **5 discovered cases hold several Lovvedtak.** They are 50936 (36–37), 101772
  (82–83), 102903 (92–101), 103527 (136–140) and 104897 (11–17). Each act still
  resolves to its exact decision, and the other decisions stay listed in
  `other_case_decisions`.

### Failures

None. Stortinget answered all 133 requests with 200, and none was retried. All
16 sak listings and 12 publication lists parsed (`parse_error` is null on every
listing). Lovdata served both archives. A failure would appear as a row with its
error and HTTP status in `capture.stortinget`, or with its `parse_error`, and as
`incomplete`, `unlisted`, `unresolved` or `contradicted`, never as a dropped row.
All 52 laws are kept in every case.

## Comparison with known ground truth

- **#617 labels.** 51/51 sak ids are rediscovered without reading them, and 52/52
  decisions agree with the header. Both sides come from Stortinget-run sources,
  the API here and web pages in #617. Agreement shows that the two routes
  agree. It does not independently prove that either is right. The law edge, from
  Lovdata, is the independent half.
- **#618 corrective re-sanction (LOV-2025-06-20-67, sak 101001).** The header cites
  Lovvedtak 41 (2024–2025). Sak 101001 carries exactly that one typed decision, and
  the chain is `attested`. The earlier, erroneous sanction LOV-2025-04-25-13 is not
  one of the 52 and was not read. No source read here says which act corrects
  which. The `corrective_resanction` relation is recorded as `unsupported`, and the
  two sanctions stay distinct events.
- **Sak 103533 source discrepancy (LOV-2026-03-06-8).** The header cites Lovvedtak
  26 (2025–2026). Discovery finds sak 103533, whose `sak_sesjon` is 2024-2025,
  through the previous session's listing, and verification attests it. The sak
  XML has `<status>behandlet</status>`, no law number, and no "Ikke gjennomført"
  text. The discrepancy #617 found is on the HTML case page, which this study
  did not read. The XML route can neither confirm nor refute it.

**Replay:** `tests/integration/test_study_52_replay.py` rebuilds every derived
result from the cached bytes and compares it byte for byte with the committed
JSON and CSV. It passed locally on 2026-11-02. Without the cache it **skips**, and
the skip reason states that the replay was not run. A skip is not a pass, and CI
has no cache.

```bash
uv run python scripts/study_52.py capture   # network: rewrites the cache and manifest
uv run python scripts/study_52.py build     # offline: rewrites the JSON and CSV
uv run pytest tests/integration/test_study_52_replay.py
```

A new capture changes timestamps, and it changes digests where the upstream bytes
changed.

### Source drift

- **Lovdata.** The 2001–2025 archive digest and `lastModified` match the pilot's
  capture of 2026-11-01. The member digests and header decisions of the two acts
  shared with the pilot (LOV-2009-06-19-107, LOV-2025-06-20-67) are identical.
- **Stortinget, pilot against study.** Saker 101001 and 43206 are
  `drift_in_bytes_only`. Their digests differ and their parsed identity (sak,
  session, typed decisions) is the same.
- **Stortinget, discovery pass against verification pass.** 51 saker were fetched
  twice, and all 51 are `identical`. This does **not** show that the source is
  stable. In 56 of the 133 captures, the body's `respons_dato_tid` is more than
  60 s older than the retrieval time, so the server answered from its cache. The
  two passes were minutes apart and most likely received the same cached
  response. Compare captures by parsed identity, as the pilot recommended.

## Limitations

- **The sample skews recent.** 34 of 52 acts are from 2025–2026, and 3 predate
  2013. Before 2013, the pilot measured that most Lovtidend headers cite no
  Lovvedtak (279 cite one, 1,074 none). This study says nothing about that period.
- **One Besl. O. act.** Odelsting decisions (until 2009) are typed in the header,
  but neither discovery nor verification resolves them. The source recon of
  2026-11-02, not this study, read `beso-200809-NNN` ids in the 2008-2009
  Lovvedtak publication list, so a later extension looks possible.
- **Listing lag.** Only S and S−1 are searched. A sak opened two sessions before its
  decision would come out `not_found`. None of the 51 needed it. Across the 16
  cached sak listings (2010-2011 to 2025-2026), there are 1,570 `henvisning`
  mentions of a Lovvedtak (a range counts once) of the listing's own session, 294
  of the next session, and 1 of the session after that (in the 2016-2017 listing). This was counted from the cache
  with a separate one-off script that is not committed.
- **`henvisning` is the recall bottleneck.** A sak whose `henvisning` omits the
  decision is never proposed. The alternative is to fetch every sak of the session
  (about 700 requests per session). Here the publication list exposed no
  unmatched id, because all 51 were found.
- **No independent ground truth for the Stortinget side** (see above). The labels
  are the only comparison.
- **The 52 are the #617 frame.** This result does not carry over to amending acts in
  general, to regulations, or to acts without a Lovverk base law.

## Recommendation for owner review (recommendation only)

**GO** for the method as a research pipeline: header decision → typed, unique
Stortinget case, label-free, with S−1 listings. On this frame it reproduced
51/51 known links and produced no false link. Next step: run it on a declared,
harder sample, weighted to 2007–2015 and including acts whose headers cite
nothing.

**NO-GO** for any publication or production crosswalk until the owner decides
the items still open from the pilot. These are evidence retention for Stortinget
bytes, the artifact location, the contract v0 schema, Besl. O. scope, the #618
event model, and reconciling `docs/decisions.md` §2/§3 (ADR-0017 gate 5). The
release state stays `research_only`.
