Other clients

No tested procedure for Cursor and Codex. We have not run the setup in these clients ourselves, and we are not going to invent the steps.

Use a client that supports a connector with OAuth sign-in, or run the server yourself, as in the Claude guide. If you get one of these clients working against the hosted service — or if you cannot — tell us at kontakt@lovspor.no, and it can stand here as a tested recipe with your name on it.
