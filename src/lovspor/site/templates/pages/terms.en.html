What the service is

lovspor consists of the website lovspor.no and the hosted lovverk service at https://lovspor.no/mcp, which gives AI tools access to the text of Norwegian statutes and central regulations. It is run by Bartosz Kobyliński as a private individual and is free of charge. By using it you accept these terms.

Not legal advice

lovspor provides the text of law for reference; it does not provide legal advice. The text may contain errors, miss provisions or be out of date, and what an AI model answers using the service as a source is the model's own answer. Check against the original source before relying on the text, and seek legal help when something is at stake.

Your sign-in is personal; do not share it. Do not try to get around the quotas, overload the service or use it in a way that keeps others from using it. Accounts that are misused or threaten the operation of the service may be suspended.
